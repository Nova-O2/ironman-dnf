SECTION 5: RUNNING DISTANCE AT THE LAST CHECKPOINT (in-race run-segment DNFs)
============================================================

Run DNFs exactly as in dropout_hazard.py (main analysis, T2 rule; _common.run_position, RUN_RULES):
km_run = total_distance_km - the event's cycling-completion distance, at two decimals (last checkpoint
crossed: a lower bound of the distance run). Run DNF = in-race DNF (finish-time DNFs excluded) with
  (a) km_run = 0 and a T2 split > 0 of at most the T2 duration limit of its event type (crossed the timing
      mat at the transition exit; enters the run at km 0), or
  (b) km_run > 0 and before the event's finish distance (km_run below the event's run length).
Not run DNFs: km_run = 0 with a T2 split longer than the limit or without a T2 split (withdrew in the
transition); records at or beyond the finish distance (no interval left to run). Sensitivities of the hazard:
strict rule, (b) only; T2 rule without the duration limit, (a) with any T2 split > 0, plus (b).
The distribution locates the last recorded checkpoint, so its shape also reflects where the
timing mats are placed; dropout_hazard.py estimates the interval-censored hazard per km.
