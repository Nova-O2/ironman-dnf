*The run of each event starts at its cycling-completion distance (the modal swim + cycling distance of its finishers) and ends at its finish distance. Run-segment DNFs are in-race DNFs recorded beyond the cycling completion and before the finish distance, plus those recorded at the cycling-completion distance with a T2 split of at most the 99th percentile of the T2 splits of the official-source finishers of their event type (20.1 min in IRONMAN®, 12.6 min in IRONMAN® 70.3), who crossed the timing mat at the transition exit and entered the run at km 0 (T2 rule); the T2 rule without the duration limit also counts those with a longer T2 split, and the strict rule counts none of the DNFs recorded at the cycling-completion distance. The first interval runs from the start of the run (km 0, observed in an event when at least one such athlete withdrew there, or a checkpoint within 1 km of the cycling completion) to the first checkpoint at or beyond 1 km; events in which the start of the run was not observed contribute only to the remainder in the between-event comparison, and events without a checkpoint at or beyond 1 km (0.5 or 1.5 km in the sensitivity analyses that move the start of the run) are excluded from both first-interval comparisons and kept in the per-kilometre curve. Panel A also excludes the events without a checkpoint at or beyond 1 km (1 IRONMAN® and 5 IRONMAN® 70.3 events). Hazard = DNFs whose last recorded checkpoint is the interval start ÷ (athletes at risk × interval width in km). Athletes at risk = finishers + finish-time DNFs + run-segment DNFs recorded at or beyond the interval start. The per-kilometre contrast excludes each event's terminal bin (the last 1-km bin of its run, which ends at or contains its finish). The recorded distances of events whose finishers' distances do not form a usable course are not used. Events stopped or recorded wholesale as DNF (in-race DNFs in at least 40% of starters) are excluded except in the sensitivity analysis that keeps them. A contrast based on fewer than 10 events in which the start of the run was observed is flagged as underpowered; none was.*
